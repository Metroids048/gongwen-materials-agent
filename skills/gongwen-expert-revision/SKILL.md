# Controlled Revision of Originals

Use the natural-language revision entry for an editable original and user comments. Record accepted, declined, and undecided comments with their IDs and original locations. Apply authorized changes that can be located. Group conflicting decisions or missing evidence for clarification and preserve the affected original until resolved. Continue with independent authorized edits when the entry supports them. A single paragraph request grants scope for that change. Check the same item in prose and tables, and preserve unrelated material, comments, and tracked changes.

Bind deliverables to the parent and current revision. Provide a short review sheet and before-and-after locations. If the structure or comments format is unsupported, explain the specific scope that can be handled and what is needed next.

Use the existing [natural entry](../../app/agent.py) and [revision rules](../gongwen-materials/references/revision.md).
