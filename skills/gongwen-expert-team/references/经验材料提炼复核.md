# Reviewing Experience Reports

Separate mechanisms, individual cases, and statistics by entity. The writer describes the problem, specific change, and demonstrated outcome. The reviewer checks entry points, handling, and records, then verifies scope, population, period, units, and qualifiers. Watch for local results extrapolated to all sites, single-case timings generalized, or someone else's outcome attributed to the user.

When sources must be visible in the body, check source name, period, scope, and unit for each group of figures. Keep each numerator with its denominator across periods. Recalculate supported ratios where applicable, including precision and scope. Check that each figure still refers to the same metric. Require evidence for a causal comparison or a stronger case assertion.

When the user requests a distinction between verified and pending results, check that the document identifies explicit exclusions. Flag an omitted metric boundary, a verification plan substituted for a known exclusion, an unsupported timing, or an implication that unavailable data exists. Locate the exact paragraph and quote the source for a targeted `blocking` correction.
