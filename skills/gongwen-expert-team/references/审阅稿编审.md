# Reviewing Drafts by Document Type

Compare each requirement against the original evidence and the draft. For briefs, check overall coverage, visits, and feasible verification. For speeches, check deployment, follow-up, responsibilities, and oral readability. For approval requests, check the single decision sought, the figures, any source approval, and drill status. For minutes, distinguish the meeting involved, hearing, submission after revisions, and passage by vote. Locate each issue by paragraph and source. Retain confirmed items and leave unsupported names, dates, figures, and approvals open.
