# Reviewing Notice Sessions and Required Details

Check each session field and its attendance conditions against the source, and fix mixed-up rows. Verify computed calendar dates from the same event and stated offset. Escalate conflicting schedules or an actual scope decision as a pending business question. An optional missing contact or signing date can be left open in an editable draft.
