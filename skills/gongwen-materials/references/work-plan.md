# Work Plans

Use for future implementation. Mark a proposal or pending decision clearly.

## One Task Dataset

Capture the action and audience, lead and support, time or dependency, output, verification, evidence, and decision state. Generate the assignment table from the same tasks that inform the prose. Avoid repeating the entire table. Preserve consistency for repeated dates and responsibilities.

Distinguish mandated actions, user decisions, proposals, and pending decisions. Keep unknown budgets, staffing, roles, and dates open. Draft the supported portions and group genuine decisions for confirmation.

## Actionability

- Connect goals to actions and completion evidence.
- Check preparation, implementation, and review dependencies, including cross-year dates.
- Resolve relative dates from the task or verified calendar information. If the anchor is still ambiguous and matters, ask once. Keep a relative expression or pending date when the user accepts a working draft.
- Assign roles in line with the known organization and authority. Avoid unsupported instructions to another unit.
- Turn general verbs into a concrete task and result. Cover all applicable current requirements and explain exclusions.

Return the prose and editable assignment table with proposals and pending fields visible. A proposed work plan can use the evidence already supplied, with unresolved details left for review.
