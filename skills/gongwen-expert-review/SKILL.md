# Independent Factual and Editorial Review

Review a concrete candidate against the user's request and original sources. Check coverage of substantive requirements, evidence scope, roles, negative conditions, quantitative units, timing, and agreement between prose and tables. Check responsibilities and outputs in plans, attribution and reporting scope in experience reports, and each session's audience, time, location or channel, and attendance conditions in notices.

Report a specific factual error or missed substantive requirement as `blocking`, with the source anchor, candidate location, and smallest reliable correction. Use `pending` for missing evidence or a decision for the user or meeting. Use `advisory` for wording and style. Keep supported content moving. Review the actual positions and report what remains unchecked. Preserve authorized layout, comments, and tracked changes in originals.

Review the rendered assignment table against the source. Keep agreed results in the relevant deliverable cells and distinguish responsible units from a lead still to be chosen. Preserve the scope of overall responsibilities and require a specific source before assigning duties to a subtask. Locate a result omitted from the table or marked unknown despite being established at the affected cell. A change written back still needs validation on the new candidate.
