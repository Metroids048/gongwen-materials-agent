# Work Plans and Pre-Meeting Consultation

Use `plan-draft` for what will be done, by whom, when, and with what deliverable. Present established actions as `fact` and proposed actions as `proposal`. Keep undecided fields open in the task table. Ask only when a decision would substantially change the plan and cannot be safely left for the meeting or represented as an option.

- Use a dependency from the source in the timing field where appropriate, such as 「预审通过后」「签订协议并登记后」「递交后」. A known dependency does not establish a deadline or owner. Use published reference processes for local proposals only. Mark those passages `proposal` and actions `proposed`. Reference-only sources cannot serve as factual anchors. Put optional reference attribution in the notes.
- Put the decisions to be made at the start of a consultation draft. Give each item decision criteria and unresolved issues. For separate decisions by multiple units, list each unit in the prose and give it a separate table row and blank decision cell. Keep all criteria for setting a date. Separate the meeting's date decision from launch prerequisites. Preserve parallel preparation steps without adding approval gates.
- For authorization, intake, fulfillment, and feedback, identify decision authority and handoff and termination conditions. Operational assistance does not confer approval authority.
- In each handoff task, include sender, recipient, records, completion criteria, and retention responsibility when known. Put a required shared record in the operational row's deliverable even when a separate retention row exists. Keep the sequence of verification, acknowledgement, and status update. In exception handling, identify the recorder, reviewer and supporting party, when closure must wait, and where the result goes. Leave the filing party open if unknown. Explain who sends records, who compiles and reports them, and who confirms the result.
- If the source establishes the unit and matters it confirms, put 「〔所确认事项〕的确认结果」 in the deliverable field. Leave a missing record format open. Do not add an approval, minutes, or a formal decision.
- Preserve required steps between authorization and filing, including preparing the service plan and helping complete materials where the reference provides them. When the source mentions publishing or updating an authorized service list, add the distinct preparatory task 「编制、公开并按需调整代办事项清单」 with deliverable 「公开的代办事项清单」. Outside practices are proposed, with unknown leads and dates left open. Intake verification is a separate action. Keep all core steps: assistance intake, eligibility checks, signing and registration, service plan, submission and tracking, result notification, normal closure and archiving, and early termination records.
- If both assistance and delegated handling are requested, include a section titled 「帮办与代办情形对照」 immediately after defining the boundary. Use one paragraph per row formatted 「情形｜仅帮办｜需办委托｜判定与下一步」 without a header. Compare the requesting and submitting parties, voluntary authorization, scope and preliminary review, signing and registration, submission, feedback, archiving, and termination. Distinguish guidance or accompanying the applicant from submission on the applicant's behalf.
- If the list and preliminary review are prerequisites, put three outcomes in the 「事项范围与材料预审」 row's 「判定与下一步」 cell. Sign once prerequisites are met. For out-of-list matters, propose guidance so the applicant can submit independently. For an unsuccessful preliminary review of an in-list matter, defer signing and help correct curable deficiencies before another review. Keep service assistance separate from approval.
- If the user asks for item-by-item decisions, a discussion table, or supplies unresolved items in an assistance/delegation consultation, add 「会商议题表」 after the pending items. Use rows 「议题原名｜待定内容｜议定要点」 with full-width separators. Follow the original names and order, with no header, numbers, or result column. For multiple units deciding separately, replace the collective row with adjacent 「议题原名（单位原名）」 rows. State what remains undecided and frame a practical decision. The host adds row numbers and blank decision cells.
- Put supported agreements, logs, and termination records in their corresponding deliverables. Avoid invented durations, coverage targets, or paperwork. If the source requires a termination form but is unclear about ordinary closure, say that early termination involves the form and archiving. Preserve result feedback and record organization for ordinary closure. Include 「正常办结是否需填写代办委托终止单，待核」 in the ordinary closure deliverable and an equivalent sentence in the prose and review notes.
- Review duties and deliverables row by row. Check that the same action, time, and scope agree across prose and task tables.
